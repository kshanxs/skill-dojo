# Style Modes and Pattern Catalog

The goal is writing that sounds like its author and says exactly what the author meant. Removing patterns is secondary.

## 1. Style Modes

### Creative / Fiction
Protect the narrator's voice, dialect, and deliberate fragments. Sharpen what is on the page, and do not add events, feelings, or imagery the author did not write unless asked to expand. Dialogue is the characters' speech: leave its grammar alone.

### Academic-Formal
Precise, measured, and free of contractions unless the field allows them. Keep every hedge, citation, and technical term. Never make a claim stronger or weaker than the original.

### Non-fiction / General (default)
Clear, direct, and specific. Prefer active verbs when the actor matters. Add an analogy only if the author asks for one.

### Conversational / Casual
Contractions, shorter sentences, and a warm tone. Keep it sounding like this person talking, not like a generic friendly voice.

### Proofreading / Correction
Fix only objective errors: spelling, grammar, punctuation, and consistency. Do not change word choice, rhythm, tone, or structure. **Leave em dashes and other stylistic punctuation alone** unless they are used incorrectly. Log every change. Query anything uncertain instead of changing it.

## 2. Pattern Catalog

Each pattern below also appears in plenty of good human writing. One instance proves nothing. Fix a pattern when it recurs, or when the sentence works better without it.

### Empty significance
"Stands as a testament to", "plays a crucial role", "marks a pivotal moment". Say what actually happened or changed.

### Promotional language
"Vibrant", "groundbreaking", "stunning", "world-class". Replace the adjective with a fact, or cut it.

### Vague authority
"Experts say", "studies show", "many believe". Name the source. If none exists, flag the claim. **Do not invent one.**

### Trailing -ing phrases
"…, highlighting the importance of…", "…, ensuring lasting impact." These add commentary with no content. Cut them or make a real claim.

### Not X, but Y
"It's not a tool. It's a movement." Keep this only if someone really believes X. Otherwise state Y.

### Automatic triplets
"Fast, reliable, and secure." Use the number of items that actually exist.

### Em dash habit
Dashes used in place of every comma, colon, and period. Default limit is two per paragraph in style modes. Project settings override this.

### Throat-clearing
"It is important to note that", "Here's the thing", "At its core". Start with the point.

### Tidy closers
A last line that restates the paragraph as a slogan. End on the last real idea.

### Assistant residue
"I hope this helps", "Great question!", "As an AI", "Here is the revised version", knowledge-cutoff disclaimers, and unfilled `[insert]` placeholders. Always remove these from finished prose.

### Formatting habits
Bold scattered through sentences, a heading on every short section, or bullet lists where the ideas need connecting prose. Match the medium.

## 3. Keeping the Voice

Before editing, note the author's habits: sentence length, contractions, person, humor, favorite constructions, and how formal they are. A style guide or voice notes in the project outrank this file.

- Do **not** add opinions, feelings, anecdotes, first-person statements, or humor the author did not write.
- Do keep quirks that are consistent and clearly intended.
- If the text is flat and needs the author's input, ask a question or leave a note. Do not invent the missing detail.

## 4. When to Leave Text Alone

- Quotations, legal language, standards, API references, and data.
- Text that already works. Report "no change needed" when that is the honest result.
- Patterns the user wants, as in satire, parody, or examples of bad writing.

## 5. About AI Detectors

This skill improves writing. It does not measure AI authorship, and it does not promise any detector result. Detectors are unreliable, and their results should not be treated as proof. If the user's goal is to hide AI use where disclosure is required, such as coursework or a publisher's policy, say that this skill will not help with concealment and offer an honest edit instead.
